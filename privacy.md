# Privacy Policy for “Pregnancy”

_Last updated: November 4, 2026_

## Introduction

“Pregnancy” (“we”, “us”, “our”) is a mobile application designed to help you track your pregnancy week by week, including due-date and fetal-development information, mom-care tools (such as Kegel exercises and weight tracking), a kick counter, a contraction timer, and a symptom/mood journal.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and handled when you use the app.

This Privacy Policy applies to all users, regardless of the app language.

---

## Information We Collect

### Data You Provide

The app allows you to create a pregnancy profile and store information such as:

- Your last menstrual period date and cycle length (used to estimate your due date and current week)
- Weight entries and belly measurements
- Kegel exercise sessions
- Kick counter and contraction timer sessions
- Symptom, mood, and journal entries

All such data is created and controlled by you.

---

### Analytics Data

We use **Firebase Analytics** and **Firebase Crashlytics** to collect **anonymous usage analytics and crash reports**, including:

- App feature usage
- Screen views
- Actions such as adding or deleting records
- Aggregated counts, non-identifying flags, and crash diagnostics

**We do not collect or send** the following data to analytics services:

- Your pregnancy profile details (last menstrual period date, due date)
- Weight, measurement, or journal entries
- Health or symptom records
- Any personally identifiable information

---

## How We Use Information

We use information only to:

- Provide and operate app functionality
- Improve app stability and user experience
- Understand feature usage in aggregate
- Deliver reminders and notifications (if enabled)

We do not sell or share personal data with third parties.

---

## Data Storage and Security

- App data is stored locally on your device
- Optional backup and synchronization may use **Apple iCloud**, if enabled by you — this data stays in your own private iCloud account and is never shared with other users through the app
- We do not operate our own servers for storing personal or pregnancy-related data

---

## Data Deletion

You may:

- Delete records or your pregnancy profile directly within the app
- Remove local data by deleting the app
- Manage iCloud backups through your Apple ID settings

---

## Medical Disclaimer

“Pregnancy” is an informational and organizational tool. It is **not a medical device** and does **not provide medical advice**, diagnosis, or treatment. Features such as the weekly development content, contraction timer, and Kegel guidance are for informational purposes only and are not a substitute for care from a qualified healthcare professional. Always consult your doctor, midwife, or other qualified provider regarding your pregnancy and health.

---

## Other Apps

After your estimated due date passes, the app may show an option to open or install our other app, “Malyatko” — a baby tracker by the same developer. This only opens a link (to the App Store or to the other app, if already installed) and does not share any of your data with it.

---

## Children’s Privacy

“Pregnancy” is intended to be used by adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
